## Who fits

Developer tools, privacy and security products, and Mac apps that respect their users.

Not accepted: cleaners or "optimizers", crypto, affiliate VPN deals, and anything that tracks people.

## Independence

- **Never inside the app.** Mac Sai has no ads and no telemetry, and no sponsor changes that.
- **No tracking.** Links go straight to your site, with no tracking pixels or redirect services. A plain landing page such as `example.com/macsai` is welcome.
- **No influence on the project.** Sponsorship does not change what Mac Sai detects, recommends or removes.
