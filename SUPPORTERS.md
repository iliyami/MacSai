## Memberships

| Membership | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **The Coffee** | Your name on this page and in the app, behind-the-scenes dev posts, and the app stays free and open for everyone |
| **The Regular** | Everything in The Coffee, plus an early "what's next" post before each release, your bug reports and questions at the front of the line, and a vote in supporter polls |

Prices and sign-up are on the [membership page](https://buymeacoffee.com/iliyami/membership). One-time coffees are welcome too: [buymeacoffee.com/iliyami](https://buymeacoffee.com/iliyami).

## How the list works

- **Opt-in.** When you join, reply with the name you'd like shown (and a link, for Power User and Team). Members who stay private are never listed.
- **Names in the app, links on GitHub.** The app shows names only, with no links or logos, so it stays free of ads. Links appear on this page and in the README.
- **Links are reviewed.** No cleaners or "optimizers", no crypto, no affiliate deals, nothing that tracks people.
- **Updated with each release.** A new name reaches the app in the next version, and this page sooner.
- **Separate from the sponsor slot.** Team members get a thank-you listing by name. The single logo placement at the top of the README is the [sponsor slot](SPONSORS.md).
